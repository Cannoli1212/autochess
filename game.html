Autochess — 7-Round Mode

Seven rounds. You hold a small hand (3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5, 6 cards by round) and play most of it (2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5 by round) onto your zone — drag a card, or tap a card then tap a square (works on touchscreens, where dragging doesn't). Don't like your hand? Redraw it (whole-hand reroll, 2× per round) before you place. Your one unplayed leftover carries into the next round. The shared community board grows like poker — flop, turn, river — and its cards count toward everyone's synergies. Win rounds to steal chips (more survivors = more stolen) — most chips after round 7 wins.
